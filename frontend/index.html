3. Preprocessing - landmarks

ctrl/cmd-click to place a point, alt-drag to move one. pick in this order:

  1. nasion bridge of the nose, between the eyes not picked
  2. left tragus small cartilage flap in front of the left ear canal not picked
  3. right tragus same, right ear not picked

4. Clipping

the yellow plane is the cut. the arrow points into the half that gets kept. pick an axis, then slide to move the plane.

or ctrl/cmd-click the mesh to move the plane to that point.

5. Mesh cleanup

vertices

leave it at 10000 if you're not sure - that's what the pipeline was validated with. uncheck if you want to keep the mesh's original vertex count.
