3. Preprocessing - landmarks

ctrl/cmd-click to place a point, alt-drag to move one. Pick in this order:

  1. sellion bridge of the nose, between the eyes not picked
  2. left landmark (e.g. LH tragus) small cartilage flap in front of the left ear canal not picked
  3. right landmark (e.g. RH tragus) same, right ear not picked
  4. secondary frontal landmark (optional, e.g. subnasale) becomes the clipping plane for the displayed/saved mesh instead of sellion; measurements stay sellion-based not picked

Align does rigid registration only - no repair or clipping yet. Move landmarks with alt-drag, or after alignment use adjust picks to reposition them, then align again to re-register. Start over with reset. After run pipeline, align/adjust picks lock until reset or a new file is loaded.
